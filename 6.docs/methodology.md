# SOC Investigation Methodology

1. **Understand the alert**  
   Review the rule, context, and triggering conditions.

2. **Gather evidence**  
   Collect logs, process data, authentication records, and network activity.

3. **Establish timeline**  
   Build a chronological sequence of events.

4. **Determine intent**  
   Assess whether the behaviour is benign, suspicious, or malicious.

5. **Document findings**  
   Provide clear, concise, and evidence‑based conclusions.

6. **Recommend actions**  
   Suggest containment, remediation, and detection improvements.
