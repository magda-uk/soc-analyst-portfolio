# Alert Severity Framework

This framework defines how alerts are classified in the SOC.

---

## 1. Informational
- No malicious activity.
- Baseline behaviour.
- No action required.

---

## 2. Low
- Slight anomaly.
- No evidence of compromise.
- Monitor user or device.

---

## 3. Medium
- Suspicious behaviour.
- Requires investigation.
- Possible compromise.

---

## 4. High
- Clear malicious indicators.
- Requires immediate response.
- Account or device likely compromised.

---

## 5. Critical
- Active attack in progress.
- Lateral movement or exfiltration.
- Full incident response required.

---

## Severity Assignment Criteria
- User risk level.
- Device importance.
- MITRE technique.
- Number of anomalies.
- Presence of encoded commands or LSASS access.
